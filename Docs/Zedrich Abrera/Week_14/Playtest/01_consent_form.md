## What this is

I am the developer of *Agawan Base*, a 3D mobile capture-the-flag game for two teams of
two to four. I am testing whether a person who has never played it can understand it, reach its
controls, and finish a match without help. This is a test of the game, **not** of you: if
something is confusing or hard to touch, that is a defect in my interface, and finding it is the
whole point of the session.

## What happens in the session

1. You are given the phone with the game already installed and open.
2. You are asked to do five short tasks, one at a time, while thinking aloud — simply saying what
   you are looking at and what you expect to happen.
3. I take written notes and occasionally a photograph of the screen. I do not record your face,
   your voice, or your name.

## What I record

- Task success (yes / yes with help / no), the time you took, and where you hesitated.
- Your spoken comments and my written notes about them, attributed to a code such as **T1**
  (Tester 1), never to your name.
- Photographs of the **screen only**.
- Device model, Android version, and screen size, because size affects whether the controls are
  reachable.

I do **not** record: your name, your face, your voice, your accounts, your contact details, or
anything the game itself does not store. The game keeps one local file (a player profile: the
name you type and your match record) and nothing is sent anywhere — the build has no networking
code at all.

## Storage and use

- Notes and photographs are stored on my own device and in my coursework folder for this module.
- They appear in the submitted report only under a tester code.
- They are used only to improve the game and to write up this week's assessment.
- Anything you ask me to delete before the end of the session, I delete.

## Your rights

- Taking part is voluntary and unpaid, and you can stop at any moment without giving a reason.
- You can skip any task, and you can ask me not to write something down.
- You can withdraw your data after the session by contacting me before the submission date; ask
  me for the current one. I will then remove every note attributed to your code.
