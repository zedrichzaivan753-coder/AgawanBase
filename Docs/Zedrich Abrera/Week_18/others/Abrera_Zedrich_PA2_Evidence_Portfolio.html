1. How to read this portfolio

Two rules govern this document.

Every artefact is named by path and says what it proves. A screenshot with no claim attached is decoration; an artefact listed against the claim it supports is evidence.

The unproven list in §9 is part of the portfolio. The module counts "submitting unverifiable or fabricated evidence" as a common mistake. A portfolio that lists only successes invites the reader to assume the gaps were hidden. They are written down instead.

All paths are relative to Docs/Zedrich Abrera/.
