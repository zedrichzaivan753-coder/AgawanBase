1. Declaration of authorship

I declare that Agawan Base 3D is my own work, produced for this module. I designed the game, I wrote and reviewed the code, I built the scenes and the art direction, and I wrote the documentation. Where that work was assisted — by tools, by third-party software, or by AI — the assistance is declared in §3 and §4 of this document, and it is also disclosed on the in-game CREDITS screen.

Specifically, I declare that:

  1. The game design, the rules, and the decisions about scope are mine. The rules constants in MatchManager.cs and Flag.cs are values I chose and tuned, not defaults I accepted.
  2. The source code under Assets/Scripts/ and Assets/Editor/ was written by me, with AI assistance as declared in §4. I am able to explain every file I submitted; Evidence/defense_prep.md is an index of where each answer lives in the repository, not a substitute for explaining it.
  3. The commits and tags cited throughout this submission were produced by me on this machine, in this repository. Evidence/repo_state.txt records the state of that repository.
  4. The evidence in Evidence/ and in the earlier weeks' evidence folders was produced by running this project, not assembled to look like results. Where a result is weaker than it appears, the document says so — for example the Week 14 catch screenshots, which were scripted rather than played.

2. What the game is adapted from

Agawang Base is a traditional Filipino playground game. Its rules and its name belong to that tradition and to no individual. This adaptation claims no rights over the game itself — the originality claimed here is in the digital implementation: the three-dimensional presentation, the code, the AI, the interface and the documentation.

This is why the theme is described as Philippine Games and Sports and not as an original property.

4. AI assistance disclosure

AI assistance was used on this project. It is declared here in the detail the module's responsible AI use requirement asks for, rather than in a single vague sentence.

4.1 Where AI assistance was used

Area Nature of the assistance
Documentation drafting Drafting and structuring of the weekly Markdown documents. Every factual claim, number and file reference was checked against the actual project before submission, and the numbers are command outputs rather than model recollections
Code drafting Drafting and revision of C# across the module — gameplay, UI, and the editor tooling that produces evidence (Week18Shots.cs, Week18Build.cs, the Week 17 screenshot harness)
Art and audio generation Part of the visual and audio assets were generated with AI tooling and then imported into this project
Code review and diagnosis Reading compiler errors, logs and profiler output to locate causes

4.2 Where it was not used, and what remains mine

  • The examination is mine. E3 is sat under supervision and Evidence/e3_record.md is deliberately blank until then. Evidence/defense_prep.md contains pointers to files and commits and **no drafted answers**, because the module treats reading a generated answer during a defense as a violation, and I agree with it.
  • The design decisions are mine. Scope, the rules, what was cut for time, and the arguments in release_record.md §2 about not rebuilding the binary are positions I took and can defend.
  • The verification is real. Tests were run, profiling was measured, screenshots were captured from a running build. AI did not produce the numbers; it helped write the code that produced them.
  • Every file I submit, I can explain. Where I cannot yet explain something, it is on the unproven list in Abrera_Zedrich_PA2_Evidence_Portfolio.pdf §9 rather than presented as finished.

4.3 What this means honestly

A reader should understand that AI materially assisted this project, and that the module permits assisted work when it is disclosed. The disclosure is not a legal formality: it is on the credits screen of the submitted build, in the project README, and here. What the assistance does not excuse is being unable to explain the code — which is precisely what the individual defense exists to test, and why no answers are pre-written for it.
