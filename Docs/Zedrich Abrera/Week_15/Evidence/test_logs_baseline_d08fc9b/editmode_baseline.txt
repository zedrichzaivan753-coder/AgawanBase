MatchSettingsTests.SetDifficulty_ClampsAnOutOfRangeValue
  difficulty must never hold a value above Hard, even if a bad value is passed in
  Expected: less than or equal to 2
  But was:  99

This failure is the defect record for DEF-15-03, reproduced rather than argued:
MatchSettings.SetDifficulty (MatchSettings.cs:181) assigns the value straight through,
while SetTeamSize (MatchSettings.cs:174) clamps and MatchSettings.Load (MatchSettings.cs:149)
clamps the very same field on the way back in. A value of 99 reaches ProfileFor(), which
falls into its else branch and silently behaves as Normal while DifficultyName() also reports
NORMAL - so the screen and the AI disagree about the preset in use.
