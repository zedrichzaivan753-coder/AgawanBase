## This is the third build of the day, and why

| # | Time | Size | MD5 | Why it was superseded |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 14:17 | 33,170,243 B | `bb36569a7a1387997c556a56aeaf0344` | `productName` carried the version and the credits line printed `0.2` |
| 2 | 14:51 | 33,170,059 B | `4b1152fe203ba6f679404d955fdaa59d` | the how-to text overflowed its box onto the BACK button |
| 3 | **15:20** | **33,170,107 B** | **`ef38de8dd65100109791002a67738da1`** | **current** |

The first two are recorded rather than deleted because each one is a real state of the release
candidate that some artefact was produced against, and a reader who finds an older hash quoted
somewhere needs to know what it was.

1. **The first build shipped `productName = AgawanBase v1.0.0-rc1`, and the credits screen printed
   `0.2`.** Both are `DEF-17-02` and `DEF-17-01` in `issue_disposition.md`: Unity derives the
   `PlayerPrefs` namespace and `persistentDataPath` from the product name, so the version in that
   string silently abandoned the saved settings and the player record, and the credits build line was
   a scene string that had been baked before the version was unfrozen.
2. **The second build was correct at the time,** and verification of it (compile check, the four
   release screens in play mode, `VERDICT: working`) turned up one defect that neither the
   accessibility audit nor the screenshot review had measured: `Panel_HowTo/Body` needed **734.5 px**
   of text in a **660 px** rect, so the last ~74 canvas units of the rules — the tail of *PAUSING AND
   SETTINGS* — rendered **behind the BACK button**. `DEF-17-07`.
3. **This build is that defect fixed.** The body box is `1560 × 600` at y `+30`, which clears the BACK
   button by 102.8 px even on a 20:9 phone where the canvas is 966 tall instead of 1031, and
   `ReleasePanelsBuild.FitText` now *measures* the paragraph against the box and shrinks the font until
   it fits, logging the result (`Body fitted at font 21: 526.2 px inside a 600 px box`). Guessing the
   font size once was enough.

Because the scenes and the panel builder changed, the scenes were rebuilt, the screenshots were
re-captured from the new layout, and the accessibility audit was re-run: **516 passed, 0 failed, 0
warnings, 24 not measurable**, identical to the pre-fix audit. `readiness_results.md` records both
runs.

## Package contents check (the DEF-15-04 guard)

Reading the archive's entry list with `System.IO.Compression.ZipFile` gives **419 entries**, and
`classes.dex` and `AndroidManifest.xml` are **at the root of the archive**; there is no
`BundleConfig.pb` and no `base/dex/` prefix. That is what separates a real APK from the Week 14 file
that was an Android App Bundle wearing an `.apk` filename: an app bundle keeps `classes.dex` inside
`base/dex/`, and no handset will install one. Build App Bundle is off in the project, and the entry
listing above is the proof for this build.

## Why this APK is smaller than the Week 16 one

Week 16 shipped a **development** build at 68.30 MB. This is a **release** build at 31.63 MB. The
difference is the development player's debug symbols and the extra runtime checks that a release
build strips out; nothing was removed from the game. No size reduction is claimed as an optimisation.

- **`84c0f2e`** is the source the APK was built from.
